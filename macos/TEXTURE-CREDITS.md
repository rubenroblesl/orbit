# HD texture credits

The Orbit release includes two community texture packs for Super Mario Galaxy in Dolphin's
custom texture format. They are used with credit and with thanks to their authors.

- HD textures by CDAMJC and Razius. <https://forums.dolphin-emu.org/User-cdamjc>
- HD HUD by Super Hackio and SY24. <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8lkzqYP7eg>

The packs belong to their authors and are not covered by Orbit's GPL license. The original game
art they replace belongs to Nintendo.
